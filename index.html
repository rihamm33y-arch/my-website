Strategy plan · Kitchen and interior design

Al-Magd

A full social media plan to turn a page of kitchen photos into a premium design brand that sells a solution, not just a look.

1.2Kfollowers when the plan began
45posts, with no consistent look
16pieces of content planned for month one

Content mix

Portfolio40%
Educational20%
Problem and solution15%
Materials and quality10%
Brand trust10%
Sales5%

What the plan covers

  • An audit of the page: mixed templates, a large watermark on every photo, and almost no reels.
  • Positioning as premium, modern and practical, with the voice of a design consultant instead of a salesperson.
  • One fixed palette of charcoal, warm beige, off-white and metallic gray, so followers recognise a post without the logo.
  • Eight reel formats, from before and after to small-kitchen fixes, each with a ready hook.
  • A caption formula of hook, value and call to action, plus daily Stories and organised Highlights.
  • Every finished project turned into five or six pieces of content.
Strategy plan · Restaurant chain, Sohag

Sheikh Rajab

A plan to turn a large audience with low engagement into orders, built around one idea: the get-together, el lamma.

78Kfollowers on the page
54Kaverage views on a sample of six reels
2branches in Sohag

Content mix

Food35%
Get-together and interaction20%
Behind the kitchen15%
Heritage and trust10%
Quality and ingredients10%
Offers and ordering10%

What the plan covers

  • An audit that found strong reach and a recognisable green, beige and orange identity, but weak engagement and off-topic content such as memes and unrelated clips.
  • A map of Sohag competitors in three circles: direct, indirect and substitutes.
  • The big idea of the get-together, under the line "el lamma tabda men hena".
  • Eight reel ideas, each ending with the same call to order on the hotline 17730.
  • Photo rules: soft daylight, close-ups of hot food and branded packaging in every shot.
  • A first-month calendar, an ads strategy, a reply system for customers and a 90-day roadmap.
